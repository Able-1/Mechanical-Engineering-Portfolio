# Solar Technician Internship

**JRB Solar Investment Limited**  
**Role:** Solar Technician Intern  
**Location:** Abuja, Nigeria  
**Period:** March 2025 – March 2026

## Overview

I completed a one-year internship with JRB Solar Investment Limited, where I gained practical experience supporting solar energy installation, troubleshooting and system monitoring.

My work covered solar street-light fault diagnosis and repair, inverter system installation, and remote monitoring of installed solar systems.

## Work carried out

### Solar street-light fault diagnosis and repair

I worked on diagnosing faults in solar street lights and carrying out repairs to restore their operation. This gave me practical exposure to troubleshooting solar lighting systems.

### Inverter system installation

I assisted with the installation of inverter systems, gaining hands-on experience with solar power equipment and installation work.

### Remote solar system monitoring

I monitored installed solar systems remotely. This introduced me to the ongoing monitoring required after a system has been installed.

## Experience gained

Through this internship, I developed practical exposure to:

- Diagnosing and fixing faults in solar street lights.
- Supporting inverter system installations.
- Monitoring installed solar systems remotely.
- Working with solar energy equipment in an on-site environment.
