# Drone flight tests

The flight tests helped us identify problems with the first prototype and make changes to the frame.

## Test 1 – Stability

**Aim:** Check the drone's stability during flight and landing.

**Result:** The report records a stable flight and landing. It also states that the MPU6050 was working and had been programmed correctly.

## Test 2 – Altitude and orientation

**Result:** The report records that the drone reached about 70 metres before we lost track of its direction and it crashed. The impact damaged one arm, the propellers and an ESC.

**Problem identified:** It was difficult to distinguish the front of the drone from the rear while it was in the air.

**Change made:** We replaced the damaged arm and used different colours on the front and rear arms to make the drone's orientation easier to recognise.

## Test 3 – Further flight test

**Result:** The drone was reported to be stable and responsive, but it crashed during landing. The report attributes this to limited piloting experience and damage to the frame and hardware.

**Change made:** We developed a revised body design known as “Blue Beetle”. The aim was to improve protection for the main components and make the drone's direction easier to identify.

## What the tests showed

The tests showed us that getting the drone airborne was only part of the work. We also had to consider orientation, landing, frame strength and how well the components were protected. We used the problems from each test to guide the next design changes.
